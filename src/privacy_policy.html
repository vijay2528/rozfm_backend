Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Welcome to Roz Fm. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect information when you use the Roz Fm mobile application, website, and related services.

1. About Roz Fm

Roz Fm is an audio entertainment platform that allows users to discover, stream, and listen to audio series, stories, episodes, and other audio content. Certain features may also allow users or content creators to upload audio, images, and other content.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Roz Fm, we may collect:

  • Name and profile information
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • User and account identifiers
  • Profile image, if provided
  • Authentication information
  • Audio files and images uploaded by users
  • Subscription and purchase information
  • Transaction identifiers and payment status
  • App usage and interaction information
  • Device and technical information
  • Crash, diagnostic, and performance information where applicable

3. Login and Authentication

Roz Fm may provide authentication methods including Google Sign-In and phone number/OTP authentication.

When using Google Sign-In, we may receive information such as your name, email address, profile image, and account identifier depending on the permissions granted.

When phone authentication is used, your phone number and verification information may be processed for authentication, security, and fraud prevention.

4. Audio, Images and User Content

Users or creators may be able to upload audio files, cover images, profile images, and other permitted content. This content may be stored on our servers or through third-party hosting and storage providers.

You should only upload content that you own or have permission and appropriate rights to use and distribute.

5. Payments and Subscriptions

Roz Fm may provide paid subscriptions, premium content, and other paid digital services.

Payments may be processed through authorized third-party payment processors or applicable app-store payment systems.

Roz Fm does not intentionally store complete card details, CVV, UPI PIN, or online banking credentials when those details are collected directly by the payment provider.

We may retain limited transaction information including:

  • Transaction or payment ID
  • Order ID
  • Subscription plan
  • Amount and currency
  • Payment status
  • Purchase date
  • Subscription validity or expiry information

6. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Provide audio streaming services
  • Manage uploaded content
  • Process and verify subscriptions and purchases
  • Maintain subscription and transaction history
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and security threats
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share or process limited information with service providers necessary to operate Roz Fm, including authentication, payment, cloud hosting, storage, analytics, notifications, security, and fraud-prevention providers.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, legal process, or valid government request.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical, administrative, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain accounts, meet legal or accounting requirements, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Certain transaction or legal records may be retained after account deletion where required or permitted by law.

10. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Roz Fm account and associated personal information using available in-app functionality or by contacting us.

After receiving and verifying a valid request, applicable information will be deleted or anonymized unless retention is required or permitted for legal, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory reasons.

11. Third-Party Services

Roz Fm may use third-party services for authentication, payments, hosting, storage, analytics, notifications, and other functionality. These providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

12. Children's Privacy

Roz Fm does not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If we become aware that such information was collected unlawfully, we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information. You may contact us to exercise applicable privacy rights.
