Privacy Policy for Roz Fm

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Welcome to Roz Fm. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how Roz Fm ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Roz Fm mobile application, website, and related services.

1. About Roz Fm

Roz Fm is an audio entertainment platform that allows users to discover, stream, and listen to audio series and other audio content. Certain features may also allow eligible users or content creators to upload audio, images, or other content.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Roz Fm, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Name and profile information
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • User/account identifiers
  • Authentication information required to sign in
  • Profile image, if provided
  • Audio files uploaded or submitted by users
  • Images uploaded or submitted by users
  • Subscription and purchase information
  • Transaction identifiers and payment status
  • App usage and interaction information
  • Device and technical information
  • Crash, diagnostic, and performance information where applicable

3. Login and Account Information

Roz Fm may provide authentication options such as Google Sign-In and phone number/OTP authentication.

When you use Google Sign-In, we may receive information made available through your Google account, such as your name, email address, profile image, and account identifier, depending on the permissions you grant.

When you use phone authentication, your phone number and related verification information may be processed for authentication, account security, and fraud prevention.

4. Audio, Images and User Content

Roz Fm may allow users or content creators to upload audio files, cover images, profile images, and other content.

Content you upload may be stored on our servers or through our hosting/storage service providers in order to provide the service.

You should only upload content that you own or have the necessary rights and permission to use and distribute.

5. Payments and Subscriptions

Roz Fm may offer paid subscriptions, premium content, digital services, or other paid features.

Payments may be processed through third-party payment processors or app-store payment systems, depending on the platform and availability.

Roz Fm does not intentionally store complete credit card, debit card, UPI PIN, CVV, or online banking credentials on its own servers when these details are collected directly by the payment provider.

We may receive and store limited transaction-related information, such as:

  • Transaction or payment ID
  • Order ID
  • Subscription plan
  • Amount and currency
  • Payment status
  • Purchase date
  • Subscription validity or expiry date

This information may be used for subscription activation, transaction verification, customer support, accounting, fraud prevention, and compliance purposes.

6. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Provide audio streaming and other app functionality
  • Store and manage user-uploaded content
  • Process and verify subscriptions and purchases
  • Maintain purchase and subscription history
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and security threats
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations
