Privacy Policy

RozFm – Audio Series Platform

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Welcome to RozFm ("RozFm", "we", "us", or "our"). RozFm is an audio series and entertainment platform available through our mobile application and website at rozfm.com.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, store, disclose, and protect information when you access or use RozFm.

By using RozFm, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use RozFm and the features you choose to use.

1.1 Account Information

When you create or use a RozFm account, we may collect:

  • Name or display name
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • Profile information
  • Account identifiers
  • Authentication-related information

1.2 Google Sign-In

RozFm allows users to sign in using their Google account. When you choose Google Sign-In, we may receive information authorized by you and provided by Google, such as your name, email address, profile picture, and Google account identifier.

We use this information to authenticate you, create or identify your RozFm account, and provide account-related functionality.

RozFm does not receive your Google account password.

1.3 Phone Number and OTP Authentication

If you sign in using your mobile number, we collect and process your phone number for authentication, account security, and account identification.

A One-Time Password (OTP) may be sent to verify that you have access to the provided phone number.

2. User-Uploaded Audio, Images and Content

RozFm may allow users, creators, or authorized account holders to upload content including:

  • Audio files
  • Audio series and episodes
  • Cover images
  • Profile images
  • Thumbnails
  • Titles and descriptions
  • Other content submitted through RozFm

We collect and store this content in order to provide RozFm's uploading, hosting, publishing, streaming, account management, moderation, and related services.

Users should only upload content that they have the legal right and authorization to upload and distribute.

3. Audio Streaming and App Activity

When you use RozFm, we may process information relating to your use of the platform, including:

  • Audio series or episodes accessed
  • Listening history
  • Playback progress
  • Playback position
  • Favorites or saved content
  • Search activity
  • Subscription status
  • Feature interactions

This information may be used to provide features such as continuing playback, maintaining listening progress, managing your library, improving the service, and personalizing your experience.

4. Device and Technical Information

Depending on the services and technologies used by RozFm, we or our service providers may automatically process technical information such as:

  • Device type and model

  • IP address

5. Subscriptions and Payments

RozFm may offer paid subscription plans, premium content, or other paid services.

Payments may be processed using Razorpay or another payment provider displayed to you at checkout.

When you make a payment, the payment provider may process information necessary to complete and secure the transaction, including payment and transaction-related information.

RozFm does not require you to provide your complete card number, CVV, UPI PIN, or banking password directly to RozFm. Sensitive payment credentials entered into a payment provider's checkout are processed according to that provider's systems and privacy practices.

We may receive and retain transaction-related information such as:

  • Transaction or payment ID
  • Order ID
  • Payment status
  • Amount and currency
  • Subscription plan
  • Purchase date
  • Refund or payment status

This information is used for subscription activation, payment verification, accounting, fraud prevention, refunds, customer support, and transaction records.

Razorpay's handling of information is governed by its own applicable privacy policies and terms.

  • Store user-uploaded audio and images
