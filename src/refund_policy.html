Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Thank you for using Roz Fm. This policy explains refund and cancellation rules applicable to subscriptions, premium content, and other paid digital services.

Because Roz Fm provides digital products and services that may become available immediately after purchase, payments may generally be non-refundable after the applicable service or digital content has been delivered, activated, accessed, or consumed, except where a refund is required by applicable law or payment-platform rules.

1. Digital Products and Services

Roz Fm provides digital services including audio series, premium audio content, subscriptions, and other digital features.

2. Subscription Purchases

Roz Fm may offer monthly, yearly, or other subscription plans. The applicable price, duration, and benefits will normally be shown before purchase.

Once a subscription is successfully activated, the subscription fee will generally not be refundable solely because the user decides not to use the service, subject to applicable law and platform policies.

3. Subscription Cancellation

Users may cancel auto-renewing subscriptions using the cancellation method provided by the payment platform through which the subscription was purchased.

Cancellation normally prevents future renewals and does not automatically generate a refund for the current paid period.

4. Eligible Refund Circumstances

A refund request may be considered when:

  • You were charged more than once for the same transaction;
  • Payment was completed but the purchased service was not activated;
  • A verified Roz Fm technical problem prevented delivery of the service;
  • An incorrect amount was charged because of a verified billing error;
  • A refund is required by applicable consumer law;
  • The applicable payment platform determines the purchase is refundable.

5. Generally Non-Refundable Circumstances

Subject to applicable law, refunds may generally not be provided when:

  • The service was successfully activated and delivered;
  • The user simply changed their mind;
  • The user forgot to cancel before an auto-renewal;
  • The user did not use the subscription;
  • A device or internet issue outside Roz Fm prevented use;
  • Premium digital content was already accessed or consumed.

6. Duplicate Payments

If you believe you were charged multiple times for the same purchase, contact us with the relevant transaction details. Verified duplicate payments will be handled according to applicable payment-provider procedures and law.

7. Failed or Pending Payments

A payment may sometimes appear deducted even though Roz Fm has not received successful confirmation.

Such transactions may be automatically reversed by the bank or payment provider. If the amount remains deducted and your service is not activated, contact us for assistance.

8. Third-Party Payments

Payments may be processed through third-party payment processors, Google Play, Apple App Store, or other authorized platforms.

When the applicable payment platform controls refunds, users may need to request the refund directly from that platform according to its rules and procedures.

9. Requesting a Refund

For eligible transactions handled directly for Roz Fm, contact:

Email: rozfm@support.com

Please provide, where available:

  • Registered name;
  • Registered email address or phone number;
  • Transaction/payment ID;
  • Order ID;
  • Purchase date;
  • Amount paid;
  • Subscription or product purchased;
  • Reason for requesting the refund;
  • Relevant supporting evidence where appropriate.

10. Refund Review

We may review transaction records, subscription status, relevant technical records, applicable payment-provider policies, and applicable law before determining refund eligibility.

Submitting a refund request does not guarantee approval.

11. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will normally be returned to the original payment method where supported.

The time required for the refund to appear depends on the bank, card issuer, payment provider, app store, and payment method.

12. Promotional Offers

Promotional subscriptions, discounts, trials, coupons, or other special offers may have additional conditions displayed when offered. Promotional benefits generally have no cash value unless required otherwise by law.

13. Account Deletion

Deleting a Roz Fm account does not automatically cancel an active subscription managed by an external payment provider or app store. Users should cancel active auto-renewing subscriptions through the platform where they were purchased.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our services, payment methods, business practices, or applicable requirements.
