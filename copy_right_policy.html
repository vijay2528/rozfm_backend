Users may access Roz Fm content only for purposes permitted by our Terms and Conditions and applicable law.

3. User and Creator Content

Roz Fm may allow creators, partners, or users to submit or upload content including:

  • Audio series;
  • Audio episodes;
  • Stories and narration;
  • Music or sound where appropriately licensed;
  • Cover images and artwork;
  • Profile images;
  • Descriptions and written material;
  • Other permitted digital content.

Users generally retain ownership of content they own. Uploading content to Roz Fm does not automatically transfer copyright ownership to Roz Fm.

However, by uploading content, you grant Roz Fm the permissions reasonably necessary to host, store, process, reproduce, display, stream, distribute, promote, and otherwise make the content available as part of operating the Roz Fm service, subject to our Terms and any separate agreement with you.

4. Your Responsibility for Uploaded Content

By submitting or uploading content to Roz Fm, you represent that:

  • You own the content; or
  • You have obtained all necessary licenses, permissions, consents, and rights to use and distribute the content;
  • Your content does not knowingly infringe another person's copyright or intellectual property rights;
  • You have permission to use any third-party audio, music, artwork, photographs, scripts, recordings, or other protected material included in your content.

5. Prohibited Copyright Infringement

You must not use Roz Fm to upload, publish, distribute, stream, or otherwise make available copyrighted material without appropriate authorization.

Examples may include unauthorized copies of:

  • Audiobooks;
  • Audio series;
  • Podcasts;
  • Songs or music recordings;
  • Movies or television audio;
  • Books or written stories;
  • Paid or subscription-only content;
  • Images, posters, photographs, or cover artwork;
  • Content copied from another application, website, streaming service, or platform.

6. Reporting Copyright Infringement

If you are a copyright owner or are authorized to act on behalf of a copyright owner and believe that content available through Roz Fm infringes your rights, you may submit a copyright infringement complaint to us.

Please send copyright complaints to:

Email: rozfm@support.com

7. Information Required in a Copyright Complaint

To help us investigate your complaint efficiently, please provide as much of the following information as reasonably possible:

  • Your full name;
  • Your email address and contact information;
  • Identification of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed;
  • Identification of the allegedly infringing content available through Roz Fm;
  • The audio series, episode, creator, URL, content ID, or other information that helps us locate the reported content;
  • A description of your ownership or authorization regarding the copyrighted work;
  • Supporting evidence of copyright ownership or authorization, where appropriate;
  • An explanation of why you believe the use is unauthorized;
  • A statement that the information provided in your complaint is accurate to the best of your knowledge;
  • Your physical or electronic signature or equivalent confirmation.

8. Review of Copyright Complaints

After receiving a sufficiently complete copyright complaint, Roz Fm may review the reported content and available information.

Depending on the circumstances, we may:

  • Request additional information from the complainant;
  • Contact the user or creator who uploaded the content;
  • Temporarily restrict access to the disputed content;
  • Remove or disable access to content reasonably believed to be infringing;
  • Restore content where appropriate;
  • Take appropriate action against accounts that repeatedly infringe copyright.

9. Content Removal

Roz Fm reserves the right to remove or disable access to content when we reasonably believe that it infringes copyright, violates our policies, violates applicable law, or creates legal or security risks.

Where appropriate, we may notify the user or creator responsible for the content.

10. Counter-Notification

If your content has been removed or restricted because of a copyright complaint and you believe the action was taken in error, or you believe you have the necessary rights or authorization, you may contact us to dispute the removal.

Your response should include:

  • Your full name and contact information;
  • Identification of the removed or restricted content;
  • An explanation of why you believe the removal was incorrect;
  • Evidence of ownership, licensing, permission, or other authorization where available;
  • A statement confirming that the information you provide is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

We may review the dispute and take appropriate action in accordance with applicable law and our policies.

11. Repeat Infringers

Roz Fm may restrict, suspend, or terminate accounts of users who repeatedly or seriously infringe the intellectual property rights of others.

The appropriate action will depend on the circumstances, seriousness of the violation, applicable law, and the user's history.

12. False or Misleading Copyright Claims

Copyright complaints and counter-notifications should be submitted honestly and in good faith.

Providing knowingly false, fraudulent, or misleading information in connection with a copyright complaint may result in rejection of the request and may have legal consequences under applicable law.
